====================================================================
CITY OF MANILA BUSINESS PERMIT & LICENSING (BPLS) REGISTRATION GUIDE
====================================================================

The City of Manila no longer distributes static PDF application forms.
All Business Permits, Locational Clearances, Sanitary Permits, and Fire 
Safety Certificates are processed through the GO! Manila Portal.

OFFICIAL PORTAL LINKS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GO! Manila Main Portal:       https://www.gomanila.com
2. BPLS Business Registration:   https://bpls.gomanila.com
3. Manila City Official Website: https://manila.gov.ph

REQUIRED UPLOADS FOR NEW BUSINESS PERMIT (MANILA):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration
2. Barangay Clearance (for business location in Manila)
3. Proof of Title / Lease Contract (if renting location)
4. Occupancy Permit / Zoning Clearance (City Planning Office)
5. Comprehensive Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (BFP Manila)
6. Sanitary Permit & Health Cards (Manila Health Department)

ESTIMATED MANDATORY FEES (CITY OF MANILA):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mayor's Permit Fee: ~₱1,000 - ₱5,000 (depends on capitalized investment)
- Garbage Fee: ~₱1,200 - ₱3,600 / year
- Sanitary Inspection Fee: ~₱500 - ₱1,500
- Fire Safety Inspection Fee: 10% of all regulatory fees assessed
