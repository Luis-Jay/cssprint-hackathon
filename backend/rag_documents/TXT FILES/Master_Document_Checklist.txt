================================================================================
        CITY OF MANILA SMALL BUSINESS COMPLIANCE - MASTER DOCUMENT CHECKLIST        
================================================================================

STAGE 1: National Business Name Registration (DTI)
Agency: Department of Trade and Industry
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] (DTI-BNRS-01) DTI Business Name Registration Application Form
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : DTI BNRS Portal (bnrs.dti.gov.ph) or DTI NCRO Office
      • Filing Mode  : Online or Over-the-Counter
      • Prerequisites: 1 Primary Government ID, 3 Proposed Business Names
      • Deliverable  : DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (CBNR)
      • Estimated Fee: ₱200 (Barangay) to ₱2,000 (National) + ₱30 DST

  [ ] (ID-GOV-01) Primary Government-Issued ID of Owner
      • Type         : Personal Document
      • Source       : DFA / LTO / PSA / PhilPost / SSS / GSIS
      • Filing Mode  : Upload (PDF/JPEG) or Photocopy
      • Prerequisites: PhilID, Passport, Driver's License, or UMID
      • Deliverable  : Verified Identity
      • Estimated Fee: N/A



STAGE 2: Barangay Business Clearance
Agency: Local Manila Barangay Hall (Specific Zone)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] (BRGY-FORM-01) Barangay Business Clearance Application Form
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : Barangay Hall desk of your specific Manila district
      • Filing Mode  : Physical Filing
      • Prerequisites: DTI CBNR Certificate, Proof of Location, Valid ID
      • Deliverable  : Barangay Business Clearance & Official Receipt
      • Estimated Fee: ₱300 - ₱1,000 (varies per barangay ordinance)

  [ ] (LOC-PROP-01) Proof of Business Location Address
      • Type         : Legal Contract / Title
      • Source       : Lessor / Property Owner / Registry of Deeds
      • Filing Mode  : Photocopy / Scanned Upload
      • Prerequisites: Notarized Lease Contract + Lessor's Tax Receipt (if renting), Transfer Certificate of Title / Tax Declaration (if owned), Authorization / Consent Letter (if home-based)
      • Deliverable  : Proof of Occupancy Rights
      • Estimated Fee: Notarization ~ ₱300 - ₱500



STAGE 3: Special Local Clearances & Permits
Agency: Manila City Planning (CPDO) / MHD / Bureau of Fire Protection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] (MNL-CPDO-01) Locational / Zoning Clearance Application
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : City Planning and Development Office (CPDO - Manila City Hall Annex)
      • Filing Mode  : In-Person / Integrated in GO! Manila Portal
      • Prerequisites: Barangay Clearance, Vicinity Map / Floor Plan
      • Deliverable  : Locational / Zoning Certificate
      • Estimated Fee: ₱500 - ₱1,500

  [ ] (MNL-MHD-01) Sanitary Permit & Health Card Application
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : Manila Health Department (MHD)
      • Filing Mode  : In-Person / Online Portal Submission
      • Prerequisites: Employee Medical Results (Chest X-Ray, Stool, Urine), 1x1 ID Photos for Employee Health Cards (Yellow Cards)
      • Deliverable  : Sanitary Permit & Individual Health Cards
      • Estimated Fee: ₱500 - ₱1,200 + Lab fees

  [ ] (BFP-FSIC-01) Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) Application
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Manila Fire District Station
      • Filing Mode  : In-Person Inspection Assessment
      • Prerequisites: Compliant Fire Extinguisher (Dry Chemical / HCFC), Emergency Lighting & Exit Signage, Floor Plan Sketch
      • Deliverable  : FSIC Order of Payment / Safety Clearance Certificate
      • Estimated Fee: 10% of total regulatory charges assessed



STAGE 4: GO! Manila Mayor's Business Permit
Agency: Business Permits and Licensing Service (BPLS) - City of Manila
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] (GO-BPLS-01) Application for New Mayor's Business Permit
      • Type         : Online Portal Form
      • Source       : GO! Manila Portal (https://gomanila.com)
      • Filing Mode  : Digital Submission
      • Prerequisites: DTI Certificate, Barangay Business Clearance, Proof of Location Address (Lease Contract), Zoning / Sanitary / Fire Safety Clearances
      • Deliverable  : Statement of Account (SOA)
      • Estimated Fee: Free to apply; fees assessed based on initial capital

  [ ] (GO-SOA-01) Unified Statement of Account Payment Receipt
      • Type         : Payment Confirmation
      • Source       : GO! Manila Portal / Bayad Center / Over-the-Counter
      • Filing Mode  : GCash / Maya / Online Banking / Cashier
      • Prerequisites: Generated SOA Number
      • Deliverable  : Electronic Official Receipt (E-OR)
      • Estimated Fee: Varies by initial capital (typically ₱2,500 - ₱10,000+)

  [ ] (MNL-PERMIT-01) Mayor's Business Permit & Business Plate
      • Type         : Final License Output
      • Source       : Downloaded via GO! Manila History Tab / BPLS City Hall Counter
      • Filing Mode  : Digital Download & Physical Display
      • Prerequisites: Settled E-OR
      • Deliverable  : Official Mayor's Permit (Must frame and display)
      • Estimated Fee: Included in SOA



STAGE 5: BIR Tax Registration & Authority to Print
Agency: Bureau of Internal Revenue (Assigned Manila RDO 29 to 34)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] (BIR-1901) BIR Form 1901 (Application for Sole Proprietor Registration)
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : BIR ORUS Portal (orus.bir.gov.ph) or RDO Counter
      • Filing Mode  : Online ORUS or 2 Manual Copies
      • Prerequisites: DTI Certificate, Mayor's Business Permit or Barangay Clearance, Valid Owner ID, Notarized Lease Contract
      • Deliverable  : BIR Form 2303 (Certificate of Registration)
      • Estimated Fee: ₱30 Loose Documentary Stamp Tax (ARF ₱500 now REPEALED)

  [ ] (BIR-1906) BIR Form 1906 (Application for Authority to Print - ATP)
      • Type         : Form to Fill Up
      • Source       : BIR RDO Counter / BIR Accredited Printing Press
      • Filing Mode  : In-Person or via Accredited Printer
      • Prerequisites: Sample Invoice/Receipt Layout compliant with EOPT Act
      • Deliverable  : Official Authority to Print (ATP) Certificate
      • Estimated Fee: Included in printing press service charge

  [ ] (BIR-BPI-01) BIR Printed Invoices (BPI) or Accredited Sales Invoices
      • Type         : Physical Receipt Books
      • Source       : RDO New Business Registrant Counter or Accredited Printer
      • Filing Mode  : Physical Order
      • Prerequisites: Approved Form 1906 (ATP)
      • Deliverable  : Registered Sales Invoices / Official Receipts
      • Estimated Fee: ₱500 - ₱1,500 depending on volume (minimum 10 booklets)

  [ ] (BIR-BOOKS-01) Standard Accounting Books of Accounts
      • Type         : Hardbound Journal Books
      • Source       : Any Bookstore (National Book Store, etc.)
      • Filing Mode  : Physical Presentation at RDO for Official Stamping
      • Prerequisites: 4 General Books: Cash Receipts Journal, Cash Disbursements Journal, General Journal, General Ledger
      • Deliverable  : BIR Stamped Official Books of Accounts
      • Estimated Fee: ₱200 - ₱400 for physical books

  [ ] (BIR-2303) BIR Certificate of Registration (COR - Form 2303) & Public Poster
      • Type         : Final Tax Output
      • Source       : Assigned Manila RDO / Downloaded from BIR ORUS
      • Filing Mode  : Physical Framed Display at Business Site
      • Prerequisites: Completed Stage 5 Filing & Stamping
      • Deliverable  : Form 2303 + 'Notice to the Public' (Ask for Receipt Sign)
      • Estimated Fee: Included in filing
